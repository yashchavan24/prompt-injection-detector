How this works (v3): your text is featurized three ways — word-level TF-IDF (unigrams + bigrams), character-level TF-IDF (2–5 grams, catches leetspeak and obfuscation), and 39 heuristic signals (categorized attack lexicons, high-precision regex attack patterns, fuzzy matching against canonical jailbreak phrases, and obfuscation detectors). A calibrated classifier combines them, then explainable guardrails apply concept-question and harmless-roleplay caps plus a hard-attack floor. Scores ≥ 0.78 are BLOCK’ed before reaching the model, ≥ 0.76 are FLAG’ed for review, everything else is ALLOW’ed.
